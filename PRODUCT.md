# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users
People trying to track their nutrition, hit daily macro/calorie targets, and build healthy habits.

## Product Purpose
A gamified nutrition tracker featuring an AI Scanner for nutrition labels, daily streak tracking with visual "flame levels", and personalized coaching messages based on the user's progress.

## Positioning
An AI-powered nutrition tracker ("Coach Hoo") with gamified elements that push users towards their goals through visual streaks and automated coaching messages.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Uses Expo Camera and React Native Health Connect.

## Brand Commitments
Has existing brand assets including a mascot and app icon.
